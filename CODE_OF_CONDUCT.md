# Code of Conduct

Desk OCR is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing the technical facts.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project-specific context.
- Respect privacy; never post another person's screenshots, logs, or OCR data without permission.
- Accept maintainer decisions and disagree without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private contact or security-reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and project-related community spaces.
